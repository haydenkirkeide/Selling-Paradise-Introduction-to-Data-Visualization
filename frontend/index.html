Selling Paradise: Visualizing Prices of Miami Housing

Each circle is a house organized by its latitude and longitude coordinates.
  • To select one house, click on a house to view its basic information.
  • To select multiple, click and drag your mouse around a group of houses.

Hover over a house for details • click to select it • drag to lasso (Lasso mode) or pan (Pan mode) • scroll to zoom • press escape or click outside of the lassoed houses to erase your selection

Age Progression: Houses by decade built

Bars count houses by the decade they were built. Click a bar to highlight those houses on the map; click again to clear. Pink shows the current selection.

Ocean Distance: Price vs. distance to the ocean

Each dot is a house. Drag your cursor across the chart to select a range of distance; the houses selected are highlighted on the map. Pricing uses a logarithmic scale.

Special Feature View: Special features effect on price

Each dot is a house; bigger dots are bigger homes. Drag a loop around the dots to select those houses on the map. Houses with no special features sit in the "None" strip. Both axes use logarithmic scales.
